Phase 1 · Deal Setup
1
Seller Creates Protected Deal
Item details, taxonomy, condition, 2–4 photos, one continuous video (≤60s), and a Google Maps pickup pin. All fields validated server-side before publish.
2
₦5,000 Guarantee Held Refundable
Auto-debited from the seller's Guarantee Wallet, or paid via gateway. The guarantee is a hold, not a fee — refunded on success, forfeited on failed inspection.
3
Buyer Funds Escrow
Buyer opens the unguessable buyer link, reviews the seller's public reputation dossier, and pays into escrow with a licensed Nigerian payment partner. DealsGate never holds principal.
Phase 2 · Physical Verification
4
Agent Job Opens in Seller's LGA First Accept Wins
Job broadcast to Certified Vet Agents covering the seller's LGA and sector. Atomic row-level locking guarantees exactly one agent wins the accept — even under concurrent taps.
5
Vet Agent Inspects Item
Agent travels to the pinned location. GPS verified within 500 metres. Checklist covers IMEI, serial, condition, functionality, and any identifiers applicable to the sector.
6
Vet Agent Submits Evidence
Continuous unedited video, multi-angle photos, and a written verdict — PASS · FAIL · NEEDS_REVIEW. All media stored in S3-compatible storage, served only via signed URLs.
7
Buyer Reviews Evidence The Evidence Gate
Buyer reviews the report and either Approves (logistics unlocked, agent paid) or Disputes (mediation opens). Nothing moves — not the item, not the money — until this step completes.
Phase 3 · Logistics
8
Shipment Dispatched
Logistics partner API creates the shipment. Courier picks up the item from the seller. Tracking number issued and shared.
9
Item In Transit Shared Tracking
Buyer and seller see the exact same timeline, driven by HMAC-verified courier webhooks with replay protection. No hidden states — one journey, two lenses.
10
Item Delivered
Courier confirms delivery. A 72-hour auto-complete window opens — the buyer may confirm receipt, rate the seller and agent, or raise a dispute before it expires.
Phase 4 · Resolution & Payout
11
Buyer Confirms Receipt
Buyer confirms the item matches the inspected report, and rates both the seller and the Vet Agent. Ratings feed the public dossier and agent trust score.
12
Payment Released & Guarantee Refunded Complete
Escrow releases the seller's net payout (item minus 2% success fee). The ₦5,000 guarantee returns to the seller's Guarantee Wallet, ready for the next deal.
🛡️
Money never moves on trust alone. Every stage is logged with actor, IP, timestamp, and state transition. Payment releases only after the buyer approves the certified agent's physical verification and confirms receipt.
Failure branches — pre-published rules, not case-by-case
  • Failed inspection — buyer refunded in full, seller guarantee forfeited
  • Buyer cancels pre-inspection — item + vet + logistics refunded, service fee forfeited
  • Change of mind post-inspection — item price refunded, vet + service fees forfeited
  • Fraud / item swap — guarantee forfeited plus ₦3,500 return logistics clawback
  • Transit damage — minor — buyer accepts, seller paid in full, carrier compensates buyer
  • Transit damage — total — buyer full refund, seller guarantee returned, carrier pays both ways
