Overview

DealsGate is a protected-deal platform for Nigerian social commerce. The MVP is complete — full technical specification, 13 working product artifacts, an 11-document legal policy library, and a 10-module Vet Agent training programme, all built without external funding. This budget funds the transition from working demonstration to a live, revenue-generating pilot in Lagos.

What ₦5M Unlocks
₦5M is enough to prove the model. It hires a real engineering team, completes escrow integration with a licensed partner, certifies the first 50 Vet Agents, and runs 200 protected deals in Lagos. Those 200 deals produce the unit economics, dispute rate, and completion data that turn a working MVP into a fundable company.
What comes next. At end of Q4, DealsGate will have live revenue, real deal data, and an agent network in Lagos. That is the foundation for a Series Seed raise in 2027 to expand to Ibadan, Abuja, and Port Harcourt — and to grow the Vet Agent network to 500+.
Why This Ask Matches SVGC 2.0
  • Student-led. Founder is a Nigerian student building a platform for Nigerian students and small businesses.
  • Real innovation. Physical verification combined with escrow and evidence gating — the first of its kind in Nigerian social commerce.
  • Verifiable deliverables. Every milestone is measurable — engineers hired, deals completed, agents certified, revenue booked.
  • Job creation. Every certified Vet Agent is a Nigerian technician earning new income from skills they already have.
  • Scalable. The same model replicates to any Nigerian city, then across Africa.
