The problem
Nigerian social commerce
runs on trust that does not exist.

What buyers fear

🚨
Pay-before-delivery scams. The seller disappears after payment — or ships a counterfeit, defective, or different item.
⚖️
No recourse. Once money is sent by bank transfer, there is no practical way to reverse it. Police are slow. Courts are impractical for ₦200k deals.
🎭
Fake social proof. Follower counts, screenshots, and testimonials are cheap to fabricate. No third party verifies a seller's real history.

What sellers fear

👻
Ghost buyers. Order placed, item dispatched, buyer unreachable at delivery. Return shipping falls on the seller.
🖼️
Fake payment alerts. Screenshots of bank transfers that were never made, or reversed after dispatch.
😤
Disputes with no evidence. A buyer claims the item differed from the listing. The seller has no defence — no video, no witness, no record.
Escrow services protect the money — but not the item. Marketplaces protect the catalogue — but social sellers do not want catalogues. Nobody has built the trust layer that makes peer-to-peer trade safe for both sides.

Why we win
Four differentiators
that competitors cannot copy quickly.

01 · The evidence gate

No other Nigerian platform blocks logistics until the buyer approves a physical inspection report. Escrow protects money; we protect the item too. Competitors cannot add this without rebuilding their entire flow.

02 · Physical verification at scale

Certified Vet Agents inspect every item in person — IMEI, serial, condition — with continuous video as court-grade evidence. This is a physical network, not a feature toggle.

03 · Public seller reputation

Buyers verify any vendor's real deal history before paying — no login required. This becomes a network asset as the directory grows.

04 · Failure paths designed first

Failed inspections, transit damage, fraud, and cancellation all have pre-published rules — not case-by-case judgement. Trust requires predictability.

The structural moat
Our competitors can copy our screens in a week. They cannot copy a certified agent network in fifty LGAs — that takes twelve months of trust-building.
