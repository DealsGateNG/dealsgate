The role

You are the physical eyes of the platform.

When a buyer pays into escrow, the item they are buying has to be verified before money moves. That is your job. You inspect the item in person, capture evidence, and submit a verdict. That is all.

01

A job appears in your LGA

When a paid deal matches your coverage area and your chosen sectors, it appears in your feed. No travel beyond your set radius — you control that.

02

First Accept wins

Tap Accept. If you are the first certified agent in that LGA, the job locks to you. Only one active job at a time — no double booking.

03

Travel to the pin, inspect

The app verifies your GPS position is within 500m of the seller's pinned location. Then you run the checklist: IMEI, serial, condition, functionality.

04

Capture evidence, submit verdict

Photos, one continuous video, and a written verdict: PASS, FAIL, or NEEDS_REVIEW. Your evidence protects the buyer, the seller, and you.

05

Buyer approves, you get paid

When the buyer approves your report, your share lands in your wallet immediately. Withdraw to your bank — partially or in full — anytime.

The badge ladder

Four stages. From applicant to Super Vet.

Every stage unlocks more. You start as a Verified applicant, become Certified Eligible after training, get Certified after three successful jobs, and reach Super Vet after ten.

Stage 1

ID Verified

You submit your NIN or BVN and pass identity verification. This is the same KYC gate every DealsGate user passes — buyer, seller, or agent. It takes minutes and is required before anything else.

✓ Access to free training ✓ Profile created
Stage 2 · Certified Eligible

Training complete + quiz passed

You read all 10 training modules and score 85% or higher on the certification quiz. Every module is written specifically for the sectors you chose — phones, laptops, cameras, appliances, vehicles, bags, watches.

✓ Can accept protected-deal jobs ✓ Can accept on-demand jobs ✓ Choose up to 3 sectors
Stage 3 · Certified

Three successful inspections

Complete your first three inspections with positive buyer outcomes and you are upgraded to Certified. Certified agents get priority in the job feed and higher-value on-demand requests routed first.

✓ Priority job visibility ✓ Higher on-demand routing ✓ Certified badge on reports
Stage 4 · Super Vet

Ten successful inspections at 4.5★ or higher

The top tier. Super Vets see new jobs first, receive the highest-value on-demand requests, and their reports carry the highest evidential weight in disputes. Super Vets are featured in the public Vet Agent directory.

✓ First in every job feed ✓ Top-tier on-demand access ✓ Featured publicly
Honest filter

Who we hire. And who we don't.

Being a Vet Agent is skilled work, but it is not for everyone. Read both columns before you apply.

✓ This is for you if…

  • ✓ You are 21 or older and have a valid NIN or BVN.
  • ✓ You live in one of our active LGAs (or will soon — see below).
  • ✓ You have real hands-on experience with at least one item category — phones, laptops, cameras, appliances, vehicles, bags, or watches.
  • ✓ You have a smartphone with a working camera and can shoot continuous video.
  • ✓ You can travel within your chosen radius (up to 20km) reliably.
  • ✓ You can follow a written checklist precisely, every time.
  • ✓ You are honest — and you can refuse a bribe without hesitation.
  • ✓ You have a Nigerian bank account in your own name.

✗ This is not for you if…

  • ✗ You cannot verify your identity — the KYC gate is hard and non-negotiable.
  • ✗ You only want quick cash with no responsibility. Verdicts have consequences.
  • ✗ You would accept a payment to change a verdict. Instant permanent ban.
  • ✗ You cannot commit to at least two inspections a week for the first month.
  • ✗ You have no hands-on familiarity with any of our sectors.
  • ✗ You are not comfortable working independently at a stranger's location.
  • ✗ You cannot tolerate buyers sometimes disagreeing with your verdict.
