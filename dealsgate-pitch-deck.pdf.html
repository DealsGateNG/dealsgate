The problem

Nigerian social commerce
runs on trust that does not exist.

What buyers fear
  • Pay-before-delivery scams. Sellers disappear after payment — or ship counterfeits, defective items, or something else entirely.
  • No recourse. Bank transfers cannot be reversed. Police are slow, courts impractical for ₦200,000 disputes.
  • Fake social proof. Follower counts, screenshots, and testimonials are cheap to fabricate. No independent verification exists.
What sellers fear
  • Ghost buyers. Order placed, item dispatched, buyer unreachable at delivery. Return shipping falls on the seller.
  • Fake payment alerts. Screenshots of transfers that were never made, or were reversed after dispatch.
  • Disputes with no evidence. Buyers claim the item differed. The seller has no video, no witness, no defence.

The solution

A protected deal,
in five steps.

1
Seller creates the deal
Photos, continuous video, Google Maps pin, and a ₦5,000 refundable guarantee.
2
Buyer pays into escrow
Held by a licensed Nigerian payment partner. Buyer never sends money directly.
3
Vet Agent inspects
Certified agent travels to the pin. Verifies IMEI, serial, condition. Captures video.
4
Buyer approves evidence
The evidence gate. Logistics cannot move until the buyer approves the report.
5
Delivery & settlement
Shared tracking. On delivery: seller paid, guarantee refunded, ratings updated.
The insight
Escrow protects money. Physical inspection protects goods. You need both — and the buyer must approve before anything moves.

Why we win

Four differentiators that
competitors cannot copy.

01 · The evidence gate
No other Nigerian platform blocks logistics until the buyer approves a physical inspection. Escrow protects money — we protect the item too.
02 · Physical verification at scale
Certified Vet Agents inspect every item in person — IMEI, serial, condition — with continuous video as court-grade evidence.
03 · Public seller reputation
Buyers verify any vendor's real deal history before paying. No login required. The directory compounds as a network asset.
04 · Failure paths designed first
Failed inspections, transit damage, fraud, and cancellation all have pre-published rules — not case-by-case judgement.
The structural moat
Competitors can copy our screens in a week. They cannot copy a certified agent network in fifty LGAs — that takes twelve months of trust-building.
